The Golden Soul Run Club

Started with pizza. Kept going anyway.

  • 01
    Every Thursday, 6:00 PMWhat began as loose fun runs with a few neighborhood spots — a run, then pizza or coffee — turned into a standing weekly date once enough people kept asking for one.
  • 02
    Meet at the storeThe shop doubles as clubhouse. Show up before 6:00, leave your bag behind the counter, head out together.
  • 03
    Choose 5K, 8K, or 10KAll three loops end back at the store — for coffee, or whatever the neighbors are serving that week.
  • 04
    Bring reflective gearThe sun's down before the run ends. A light layer, water, and ID cover it — the rest is optional.
  • 05
    Free, every week, open to everyoneYou don't need to sign up to show up. It just helps us plan pace groups and know who's coming.
